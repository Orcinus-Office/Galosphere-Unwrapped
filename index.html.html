01

Crystal Glitters

✨

A new pretty decorative feature made from crystal! These delicate shards act just like pink petals in Minecraft — place them in a cluster of up to four to create shimmering underground gardens.

Feature Breakdown

  • Crafted from crystal fragments (new recipe!)
  • Place up to 4 in a single block space
  • Naturally generates in crystal caves
  • Purely decorative — no light emission, but they sparkle!
Decoration Vanilla-Friendly Crystal
02

Rock Candy

🍬

A yummy new treat for your underground explorations! Rock Candy is a food item made from crystals and sugar. It's the perfect snack to keep your hunger bar full while you dig deeper.

Feature Breakdown

  • Crafted with sugar + crystal shards
  • Restores hunger and gives a small speed boost
  • Stackable to 16 (it's candy, after all)
  • Comes in different colors based on crystal type
Food Exploration Crystal
03

Silencer

🔇

A brand-new redstone mechanic! The Silencer is a block that mutes the sounds of blocks depending on which direction it's facing. Adjust the volume using a redstone comparator signal.

Feature Breakdown

  • Directional muting — face it at noisy blocks
  • Redstone comparator input adjusts volume (0–15)
  • Perfect for quiet farms and hidden bases
  • Does not mute hostile mob sounds (sorry!)
Redstone Utility Sound Control
04

Healing Cloud

☁️

Ever since Minecraft added Potent Sulfur which exerts noxious gas when above water... why not healing gas when there's a pink salt underneath it? A breath of fresh air for the deep dark.

Feature Breakdown

  • Place Pink Salt under Potent Sulfur to create Healing Clouds
  • Provides regeneration to players who step inside
  • Particles float upwards, creating a beautiful visual effect
  • Noxious gas above, healing gas below — use wisely!
Compatibility: Planned to be fully compatible with Blackgear's Vanilla Backport.
Utility Gas Vanilla Backport
05

Atuned Hopper

⚙️

The Atuned Hopper is a new mechanical block that acts just like a hopper, except it has a filter on each slot. This allows players to precisely control exactly what items can be filtered out.

Feature Breakdown

  • Functions as a standard hopper but with per-slot filtering
  • Place items in the filter slots to specify what can pass through
  • Ideal for complex sorting systems and automated storage
  • Can be toggled on/off with a redstone signal
  • Crafting: Made from a Hopper, Allurite Shards, and Palladium Ingots.
Redstone Logistics Utility
06

Stone Plates

🪨

A simple but versatile new decorative block. Stone Plates can stack up to 16 items on a singular plate, and you can make them with any kind of stone. Perfect for displaying your treasures.

Feature Breakdown

  • Craftable with any stone type (deepslate, granite, etc.)
  • Holds up to 16 items in a single stack
  • Items visually render on top of the plate
  • Right-click to add/remove items
Decoration Storage Stone
07

Gauntlet

🛠️

A new netherite tool that acts like a multitool! It can act like a pickaxe, shovel, axe, hoe... whatever you can think of! The ultimate tool for the ultimate miner.

Feature Breakdown

  • Netherite-tier multi-tool (combines pickaxe, shovel, axe, and hoe)
  • Right-click to cycle between tool modes
  • Durability scales depending on the block broken
  • Repairable with netherite ingots
Tool Netherite Multitool
08

Glow Worms

🐛

Glow Worms are similar to glow lichen in a sense they're more like blocks than a living creature. But they serve as a beautiful light source underground which would pair perfectly with the glow squids swimming in the aquifers.

Feature Breakdown

  • Emits a soft, natural light (light level 10)
  • Can be placed on ceilings and walls like glow lichen
  • Emits small particle effects for ambience
  • Naturally spawns in underground biomes
Light Source Decoration Ambience
09

Cinderpot

🔥

Cinderpot is a new cute little mob inspired by the Flame Construct from Minecraft Dungeons II. This mob acts like a block initially but will occasionally peak out. Provoke it, and it will shoot fire at you — or unleash a fiery shockwave!

Feature Breakdown

  • Disguised as a block until it peaks out
  • Shoots fire projectiles when provoked
  • Periodically uses a fire shockwave attack
  • Adds a new threat to underground exploration
Hostile Mob Fire Underground Threat
10

Fire Pit

🔥

A new decorative block that you can craft from Cinderpot's drops (i.e. Embers). It's essentially the block version of what they actually look like, bringing a warm, cozy glow to your builds.

Feature Breakdown

  • Crafted using Embers dropped by Cinderpots
  • Matches the aesthetic of the Cinderpot mob
  • Emits a warm, flickering light
  • Perfect for underground camps and cozy corners
Decoration Light Source Cinderpot
11

Pink Salt Temple Painting

🖼️

A new painting variant that showcases the foreboding Pink Salt Temple with an ominous feel. Hang it on your walls to remind yourself of the dangers that lurk below.

Feature Breakdown

  • New painting variant with a unique 2x2 or 3x3 size
  • Features the Pink Salt Temple in an ominous style
  • Can be placed like any standard Minecraft painting
  • Adds more lore and atmosphere to your builds
Decoration Painting Lore
12

Palladium Retexture

💎

A final rework on the Palladium textures to reinforce its theme. The new textures will make the material feel more cohesive and visually distinct.

Feature Breakdown

  • Complete texture overhaul for Palladium items and blocks
  • Reinforces the theme and visual identity
  • Final implementation — no more changes after this!
  • Designed to match the aesthetic of the Galosphere mod
Retexture Palladium Final
13

Block Comparator

🔴

A new redstone component that compares if a block is the same as another, and it outputs a redstone signal based on how similar it is. A game-changer for advanced redstone engineers.

Feature Breakdown

  • Compares two blocks and outputs a signal based on similarity
  • Signal strength scales with how similar the blocks are
  • Perfect for advanced sorting and detection systems
  • Final implementation — ready for release!
Redstone Logic Final
14

Relay

📡

A new block that can be crafted with Palladium which copies the redstone signal from one end to another. Perfect for transmitting signals across long distances or through walls without losing strength.

Feature Breakdown

  • Copies and relays redstone signals from one side to another
  • Crafted using Palladium
  • Maintains signal strength over long distances
  • Ideal for compact and efficient redstone circuits
Redstone Utility Palladium
